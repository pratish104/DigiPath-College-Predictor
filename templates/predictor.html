UNDERSTAND CAP SEAT LABELS
GOPEN — an Open-category general seat. It is not a separate student category.
LOPEN — an Open-category seat reserved for eligible women candidates.
GSC / LSC — SC seats for general and eligible women candidates respectively. The same G/L pattern appears for OBC, ST, VJ-A, NT and SEBC records in this data.
GOBC / LOBC, GST / LST, GVJ / LVJ, GNT / LNT — the corresponding OBC (including SBC), ST, VJ/DT and NT general/ladies records. The DSE data also has GNTA/GNTB/GNTC/GNTD and matching L codes.
GSEBC / LSEBC and EWS — SEBC general/ladies records and Economically Weaker Section records. They apply only when the relevant CAP claim is verified.
H / O / S — Home University, Other Than Home University and State Level. They are CAP allocation scopes, not reservation categories.
DEF / DEFR (for example DEFOPE, DEFOBC, DEFRSC) — defence-reservation records; only relevant with confirmed defence eligibility and the stated category.
PWD / PWDR (for example PWD-O, PWDOBC, PWDR-SC) — disability-reservation records; only relevant with confirmed PwD eligibility and the stated category.
EWS, TFWS, ORPHAN, MI — EWS, Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme, orphan, and minority records that occur in the bundled engineering data. Select them only when your CAP application/documents establish that eligibility.
