UNPREDICTABLE CAP ROUND CUTOFFS

MHT-CET & Diploma (DSE) CAP round cutoffs shift dramatically each year due to seat availability, category dynamics, and state-level policy changes — leaving thousands of students unable to target colleges effectively, missing seats by tiny margins.

INVISIBLE ATS KEYWORD BARRIERS

Over 75% of resumes submitted by engineering freshers are auto-rejected before any human reads them. Applicant Tracking Systems silently filter out profiles missing domain-specific tech keywords — a critical gap most graduates don't know exists.

Unverified companies request security deposits, demand personal documents upfront, and circulate fabricated offer letters targeting desperate final-year students. Reported placement fraud in Maharashtra has risen over 40% in three years.
